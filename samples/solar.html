FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

How long does installation take?

Most home systems are installed in one or two days. Paperwork and switch-on usually take a few more weeks.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. We work with partner banks to offer easy monthly plans for homes and businesses.

What happens on cloudy days?

Panels still produce power in diffuse light, and you draw from the grid whenever you need extra.

Is maintenance included?

Every system includes free cleaning and a health check in the first year, plus remote monitoring.
